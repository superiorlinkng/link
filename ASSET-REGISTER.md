## Publication process

Keep counterparty letters, identity records, signatures, bank details, commercial agreements and pricing evidence out of this public repository. Store originals in an owner-approved private location. For public documents, confirm authenticity, currency, permission, redaction and factual claims before adding a reviewed version. Track approver, review date, expiry and public filename. Do not treat robots.txt or NDA text as access control. Removed files may remain accessible from historical deployments or Git history; this PR does not revoke that access.

CNG inquiry requirements

CNG means Compressed Natural Gas. Describe the intended use, location, expected demand and existing receiving or refuelling setup. Infrastructure and location affect supply and offtake planning.

Does an inquiry confirm supply?

No. Product availability, specification, logistics and commercial terms must be confirmed in writing for each requirement. Do not send identity records, bank details or confidential contracts through the public inquiry form.

What to include in a haulage inquiry

Product and quantity

Identify the product, total quantity and unit, loading date, and whether you need a single movement or a repeat schedule.

Loading and destination

Provide the loading point, destination, site access conditions and receiving window so the team can review routing and delivery arrangements.

Documentation

Explain the loading records, waybills and proof of delivery you require. Vehicle, driver and site requirements are reviewed before a movement is agreed.

Planning by corridor

Our existing corridor information covers Lagos and Ogun, Abuja and North Central, and South East Nigeria. A listed location does not guarantee current transport capacity or availability.
